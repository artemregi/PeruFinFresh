§ 4. Garantía en criptomonedas

El valor de la garantía se calcula en base al precio de mercado actual de la criptomoneda, aplicando el coeficiente LTV (Loan-to-Value) establecido por FinFresh.

En caso de caída significativa del valor de la criptomoneda que constituye la garantía, FinFresh tiene derecho a:

  • Exigir al Prestatario que reponga la garantía (margin call).
  • Exigir el pago anticipado total o parcial del préstamo.
  • Vender la garantía para cubrir la deuda, previa notificación al Prestatario.

Tras el pago total del préstamo junto con los cargos correspondientes, la criptomoneda de garantía queda desbloqueada de inmediato y queda disponible para el Prestatario.

§ 4. Cryptocurrency collateral

The condition for granting a loan is that the Borrower transfers the required collateral amount in cryptocurrency to the escrow address specified by the Service. The collateral value is calculated based on the current cryptocurrency market price, applying the LTV (Loan-to-Value) ratio set by FinFresh.

If the value of the cryptocurrency comprising the collateral drops significantly, FinFresh has the right to:

  • Require the Borrower to top up the collateral (margin call).
  • Require early full or partial repayment of the loan.
  • Sell the collateral to cover the debt, after notifying the Borrower in advance.

After full repayment of the loan along with applicable payments, the collateral cryptocurrency is immediately unlocked and made available to the Borrower.
